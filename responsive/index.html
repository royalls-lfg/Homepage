    • The capybara[a] or greater capybara (Hydrochoerus hydrochaeris) is the largest living rodent,[2] native to all countries in South America except Chile. It is a semiaquatic herbivore that inhabits savannas and dense forests, living near and in bodies of water and feeding mainly on grasses and aquatic plants.
