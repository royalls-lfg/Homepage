Professional kit 8.5x11 and _x_ spiral bound inkjet book 2.5x3 inches laser business cards Multi Studio Project Description: A leave behind is a physical object that you leave behind after an interview with a potential employer or meeting with a potential client to remember you by. It can be anything — a branded pen like banks give out, a flip book, an ornament, a balloon — but is usually representative of you as a person, designer, and potential employee. A business card provides potential clients / employers / collaborators with your contact information. It induces your name, email, and a link to your website or portfolio. It is typically 2D and can fit into a wallet. Artist Statement: I wanted to leave my future employer less stressed and fully happy to relax and understand my goal as a designer. Many employers and employees get caught in the chaos of day to day life and stresses. I wanted to bring a fun and enjoyable experience to show off my fun designs, while also expressing my love for self care, and the ability to share with others! I wanted to display this for my leave behind but also embellish my business card in a fun, relaxing, nature inspired way as well! My brand being formed from my name “Sydney Raye Royall” and my design title being raye royall designs. Using the meaning of my names I created a logo based off of a crown, ocean scene, and sun rays!
