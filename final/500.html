500 page book 8x6 inches vellum and laser Core 2 Project description: Find an object to speculative write a past, present, and future world. Create a 500 page book that represents this through production and crafting elements. Artist Statement: My item was a cherub. I started off with a speculative narrative of the cherub for the past, present, and future. I then intertwined with my personal realizations of past experiences and kept going back and forth in dream-like scenarios. I wanted to show a messy process book of the inner workings of mental processings of childhood. I was in the present, realizing about the past and questioning the future. Using my own photography of nature and spiritual elements found in a blue and orange hue, I wanted to represent the foreground of reality. I then edited those images into an outlined, black and white oil painting texture on separate pages to represent the perspective of a spiritual realm unreached by human nature. I positioned those pages over top while the cherubs intermingled between the two realms metaphorically and contextually between the two pages, while interacting with the photos behind on the next page. The red represents unpacking pockets of certain memories and emotions unexpectedly. Leaving a perplexed feeling and anxious reminiscence. I used a poem to guide the reader along through the narrative of the book.
