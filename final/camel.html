Om-Arth _x_ inches head scarf embroidered, sewn, and jeweled Multi Studio Project Description: A Brave New World is a group project of 5! We drew 5 limitations for our world to give us some guidelines, but after that we had to build our world from scratch. To identify this world, we needed 5 outputs from this planet to explain the way of life. These items were to be a field guide, flag, currency, and two other items of our choosing. World Building: Our world was built around modern day Morocco in the future years of 2075. A foreign material poisoned our water system, leaving most civilizations to perish but leaving those of desert living to withstand the harsh environments in lack of water. These people figured out a way to purify the water in the land through the ocean. Thus leaving water bureaus in a popular demand. Although they have power they are not absolute. This civilization is highly reverent of the camels that provide them knowledge of the past memories lost from disaster, food in drought from minimal arable land, and hope provided through the camel prophetess in which handles them. The population is guided by a matriarchal government in which everyone falls under fair treatment. If you do not contribute to the wellbeing of the society then you will be outcasted and left with no water treatment, leaving you to fend for yourself. Artist Statement: The camel prophetess is signified through this garment which protects from the environmental aspects of the sand and heat elements. Not only is it conventional, the symbolism is a pattern meant to be inspired from sacred geometry, the flower emblem in the middle represents balance with the two dots on either side and repetition of true sacred elements. Curated through a symbol of womanhood below the two humps of the camel, and the sun rising over them. The waves and dunes collide in pattern with the droplets peaking at the top. This is all on the crown of the head speaking great volumes of knowledge. On the back portion of the scarf is a symbol of water and the symbol of our world. The symbol of our world was reversed onto itself for the value of balance once again. Gold represents wealth and status covering the eyes proportionate to holding the rest of the weight in the front. Artist Statement: The field guide being one of the most important aspects in discovering the ruling and nature of our world. With the language of the people of Om-Arth being a language of the tongue and not fully composed onto script, the written english translation of the guidelines was written to be a caution tale. Laying out all of the rules of the land in a mysterious tell tale guiding the foreign passengers along into the scenery. Usually rolled into a scroll and kept for those in need of assistance finding their place in society. One of our group members created a font of our writing system which is broken into sections of the alphabet and leaving certain letters repeated in a way english speakers are not used to. Around the edges are the Om-Arth legends and translated in english in the corner and center of the scroll. Repeated elements decorate the rest of the poster and center our two humped camel!
