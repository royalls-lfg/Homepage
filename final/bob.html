“Bob’s Basement” 8.5x5.5 inches laser booklet Core 1 Project description: Find a book over 35 years old that has an interesting composition to recreate. Re-interpret or customize the book to be your own, only using the words provided in the book. Print on 8.5x11 paper and create it into a booklet using 4 pages and having 16 spreads. Artist Statement: I found a book that most would overlook. I found it to be a very junky and poorly designed book with lots of fun artifacts waiting to be seen. I wanted to keep the same elements of the original book with the retro “uncle” feeling vibe to it! I summarized the book with sentences within the guidelines, and allowed the reader to appreciate the original book for something entertaining and educational!
