Type Specimen Poster Core 1 17x11 inches Laser poster Collection of 4 Project description: The assignment was to find a typeface older than 50 years of creation and create 2 posters expressing all of its qualities. Displaying the entire alphabet, a quote from the creator, paragraph of the origin history, and the title of the typeface. The idea was to have two different variations able to be presented in black and white as well! Artist Statement: I was inspired to use “Bogart” to find a way to use a pop derived typeface and create it to the liking of my personal style. Using the bright colors that oppose each other I found interesting ways to display the experimental designs while displaying all the font has to offer!
