Mark Making 66x17 inches inkjet map Core 2 Project description: Our teacher gave us all random objects chosen from a bag. We had to then curate 100 marks using black paint on white paper. After that we had to exhaust the marking of the object yet again 100 more times, this time being able to mark with any black pigment and on ant surface. For the last marking we had to create a mark that was unconventional, whether that be a sound, 3D, or on an unlikely surface. After collecting those we had to put it into a final output that reached the diameter of 3 tabloid pieces of paper combined. Artist Statement: My object was a crystal. After curating so many conspicuous marks, Using the 3 tabloid sizes I added them all together in a vertical manner and created a map using the upclose scan of a leaf to resemble a zoomed out image of a mountain range and river formations. After folding the map one way I curated a narrative realizing the nature of nature. Being a beautiful experience of pareidolia, which is a phenomenon in which we recognize similar patterning in ourselves and nature. The other half of the map being what we can do with this information, in a comforting sense and a call to action. A questioning in a 3rd perspective view without judgement but with a moral obligation we have to this earth and to each other.
