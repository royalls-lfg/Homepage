Serendipity Design Practices _x_ inches Risograph japanese stab binding french fold Copy 1 of 6 Project description: This was a group project assigned to four individuals in our design practices class! We all researched a segment on the future of archival spaces. How would the use of serendipity be involved? The paper must be outerly sourced and risographed for the final! Artist Statement: For my section on the group project I researched the validity of human checking systems when it came down to the release of public information regarding human bias. I referenced the book bans and certain peoples inclinations to reduce the diversity of true knowledge for an alternate agenda. Questioning the past while furthering the future with a speculative solution on how to break this cycle!
