Angel pathway Design Practices 72x10 inches accordion fold laser printed Project description: This assignment explored the documentation, organization, and synthesis of a collection! This project allowed us to obtain around 200 images of any collection of our choosing and to format it in either a booklet or an accordion fold format! Artist statement: During this time I was catching myself finding throughout my day to day life an absurd amount of repeating numbers! I found myself being curious of the importance and spiritual impact it may hold on me. I wanted to display this project as a ride throughout my life through Richmond and my home town, as if you were going down a mystical pathway with me, trying to decipher the meaning for the present and future outcomes!
